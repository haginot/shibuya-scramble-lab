A local three.js miniature of Asagaya or Koenji with OSM buildings, GSI terrain, instanced people, street activity, clock-driven lighting, and a ten-car Chūō train. Serve the repository root with `python3 -m http.server` and open `/web3d/sol/`.

URL controls:

- `area=asagaya|koenji`: defaults to `data/engine.json`’s `area`, then Asagaya. Koenji loads `engine_koenji.json`, `buildings_koenji.json`, and `terrain_koenji.json` from `web3d/data/`. Titles and station labels follow the selected data; station names come from the station POI.
- `exag=2`: vertical terrain exaggeration (0–10). Building data’s `origin` controls latitude/longitude conversion: east = `(lon-lon0)*111320*cos(lat0)`; north = `(lat-lat0)*111320`. Scene Z is south. DEM values are already metres above `base_m`, so the base is not added again. Bilinear sampling places geometry, roads/heat, rails/train, people, vegetation, and badges on the terrain. Edges clamp outside the DEM.
- `seed=4281`: deterministic procedural street trees, lamps, rooftop AC units/water tanks, and decorative people.
- `scenario=baseline|rain|event|newcafe`, `alt=bike|cafe|plaza`, `t=1108.65` (simulation minutes), `speed=1` (simulation minutes per real second), `orbit=0|1`. Default camera and interactive drag/zoom remain available. `speed=0` freezes simulation time.
- `cam=intro`: 30-second director sequence starting when `window.__ready` becomes true: 0–6s elevated broadside platform shot with arriving train and alighting figures; 6–14s rising overview with street heat; 14–22s orbit across shop badges; 22–30s high oblique wide view, held afterward. The opening targets the stopping platform on the rails nearest the station, not the station POI. It samples both sides and nearby building bounds to choose a position about 90–102m horizontally away, roughly 55–70m above local terrain and looking down about 35–40°. Clearance takes priority on taller terrain/buildings: throughout the intro the camera stays at least 45m above roofs within 60m. Every camera mode also checks footprint bounding boxes expanded by 5m each frame and raises above roof height +10m. Opening field of view fits all ten cars with margins, including portrait captures. Intro trains run on real elapsed seconds even if simulation time is paused; the first stops at 1.2s (0.65s with timelapse).
- `cam=site`: fixed close oblique view, centered on the station-front intervention and consistent across alternatives. Samples 48 directions for clear parcel sightlines, aims for 80–110m distance and a roughly 40° downward view, and stays at least 40m above roofs within 60m (roof safety takes priority). A tight 28° field of view adapts for portrait captures so bike racks, café, or plaza remain large and fully in frame. OSM footprints overlapping the intervention parcel are cleared. The floating station-corner label shows the active alternative, foot traffic/day, average dwell in minutes, and toy sales ¥/day; switching alternatives updates it immediately.
- `agentScale=1`: figure scale multiplier (0.25–6). Both recorded people and alighting passengers use approximately 3m capsules with unlit, saturated emission and additive soft halo sprites. Type colors are residents **#4fc3ff** cyan-blue, visitors **#ffb02e** orange, and commuters **#ff5fa2** pink, matching the legend and ground tokens. Figures grow with camera distance to maintain at least eight pixels of projected height, including in the overview; halos scale with the figures and retain a 16-pixel minimum.
- `timelapse=1050,1380`: simulation minutes move linearly from 17:30 to 23:00 over exactly 30 real seconds, then hold. This takes precedence over `t`, `speed`, play/pause, and time scrubbing. Endpoints clamp to 360–1440; malformed values disable timelapse. Decorative trains arrive every three real seconds, first stopping at 0.65s. Each arrival releases 96 passengers over 0.4s. Passengers move from doors onto the exposed platform edge, along to exits and down to terrain, reaching surrounding streets around 3–5s after arrival and dispersing until 7–10s. Up to five fading trail segments show the previous 0.45s of each route, capped at six metres. Up to four cohorts overlap, independently of train departure, alongside recorded people. Cohorts and trails derive from elapsed time so sparse recorder timestamps work too.
- `compare=1`: compact interface with a large bottom bar comparing all three alternatives and highlighting the active one; click a card to switch. Shows toy foot traffic, average dwell, and toy sales estimate per day.
- `hideui=1`: hides control panels, keeping shop visit-count badges, the floating station-front site label, other visible map labels, the disclaimer, OSM/GSI credits, and clock. Normal offscreen and label-overlap culling still applies. Captions remain available. Legacy `ui=0` hides the HUD while keeping map labels and credits.

Recording examples:

```text
/web3d/sol/?area=koenji&cam=intro&timelapse=1050,1380&hideui=1&agentScale=1.5
/web3d/sol/?area=asagaya&cam=site&compare=1&alt=cafe&t=1200&speed=0
/web3d/sol/?area=asagaya&cam=site&hideui=1&alt=plaza&scenario=rain&t=1320&speed=0
/web3d/sol/?area=koenji&scenario=event&hideui=1&t=1200&speed=0
```

Wait for `window.__ready === true`, then use `window.__set({scenario, alt, t, speed, playing, orbit, ui})` for immediate updates. `window.__caption('日本語の字幕', 'English caption')` shows a large two-line social-video caption, Japanese above English; `window.__caption('', '')` hides it. Text is inserted as text, not HTML. `window.__stats` exposes time, area, camera mode, elapsed seconds, scale/exaggeration, people/train activity, and rendering counts. Scene animations use RAF timestamps or `performance.now()`; no wall-clock timers or CSS transition delays are needed by a recorder.

Lighting follows simulation time: 17:30–19:00 has an orange horizon grading into a muted violet sky. By 20:30 both horizon and zenith are near-black navy. Night hemisphere ambient has a 0.19 floor (0.1615 in rain), with slightly stronger roof outlines so silhouettes remain readable after 21:00. Windows and glowing streets remain lit against the dark buildings.

Rain adds 2,400 bright slanted streaks concentrated around the camera focus, falling independently of simulation speed. Ground and road colors darken, roughness drops, and terrain-following puddles plus elongated cyan/orange additive light patches suggest reflected street lighting without a reflection render pass. Switching away from rain restores dry materials and hides its effects. Event mode places a stage, saturated crowd, colored lights, pulsing floating halo and label at the event POI in `engine.scenarios.event.extra[]`; the halo floats above nearby roofs for visibility. These are decorative scenario cues, not additional simulated agents.

The economics panel reads `toy_econ.alts` and displays `toy_total_yen` as **toy sales estimate ¥X/day (toy assumption, not a forecast)**. This combines assumed site sales, bike fees, and nearby shop sales delta; the info line displays the supplied `assumptions.formula`. Other daily totals still come from each alternative’s simulation summary. Scenarios control map activity. Recorded station departures are grouped into six-minute arrivals in normal mode; extra alighting figures, glows, and sparks are decorative. The permanent label remains: “not a prediction; a toy model for testing hypotheses”. Credits: © OpenStreetMap contributors · 標高: 国土地理院.

Verification (no browser required):

```sh
node --check web3d/sol/app.mjs
node --check web3d/sol/city.mjs
node --check web3d/sol/verify.mjs
node --experimental-vm-modules web3d/sol/verify.mjs
```

The Node harness uses real data and three.js geometry with a renderer/DOM shim and controlled RAF timestamps. It checks both areas and defaults, terrain interpolation/origins/grounding, seeded decoration, economics, comparison selection, captions, camera stages, sparse timestamps, timelapse lighting and repeated trains, controls, finite buffers, and surface winding. Capture regressions cover opening altitude/pitch and full-train framing in landscape and portrait, 45m roof clearance throughout the rise, triangle raycasts to the crowd, overlapping bursts and street dispersal, exact type colors and 3m/eight-pixel capsules, glow/trail buffers, and preserved labels with hideui. Site checks cover center/framing/size, roughly 40° pitch, close distance, 40m roof clearance and actual triangle visibility for both areas and all alternatives. Scenario checks cover falling rain with a frozen clock, wet/dry material restoration, event extra[] placement, warm sunsets, near-black sky and readable night ambient. Browser/WebGL visual verification and shader compilation are unavailable in the sandbox: the headless Chrome attempt is blocked by socket restrictions.
