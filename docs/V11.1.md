## The fixes
1. **Hachiko statue enclosure (end card, every pair):** 「10/30(金)早朝〜11/1(日)朝方」 → 「10/30(金)早朝〜11/1(日)まで（予定）」.
   EN: "Oct 30 (Fri) early AM until Nov 1 (Sun) (planned)". The start ("10/30 early morning") is unchanged. The source stays
   the Shibuya City press release (hodo_20261021: end "11/1 morning, planned, may change"). News reports say ~17:00, which
   is why the end is now given as planned.
2. **The U-turn into the Hachiko gate is the normal-day route (gate).** The over-move caption over the outdoor Hachiko-square
   plate keeps the plate line 「ハチ公口の広場、改札待ちの列」 (v10.2 rule). It now says 「平常時の動線：宮益坂口側でUターン」
   and adds 「当日は係員の指示に従う」 (EN "Normal-day route: … / On the night, follow staff directions"). The ghost says
   「ふだんはUターンで入るよ。／当日は係員の指示に従ってね」. The lesson card and the cheat sheet say 平常時 / "Normal days" too.
   **New sourced fact:** the over-result caption (from 1.6 s) now reads 「ハロウィーン当日、2024年は／宮益坂方面へ通り抜け不可／
   （2026年のJRの対策は未発表）」. Its sources are MEAS-15 = Shibuya Keizai Shimbun 18753 (2024-10-30) and NA-JR-2026. It
   replaces 「ハロウィーンは例年一部規制」, which the lesson card still carries.
3. **zone: walkers are not blocked by closures.** The 2026 police closures are for vehicles (black frame = vehicles banned,
   red frame = checks / restrictions). Every line that said walkers stop because of restrictions now names the crowd and
   the staff guidance, or says "vehicle":
   - the ghost lines over-result / over-lesson / retry-why;
   - the over-result caption 「車両の検問・規制」 and the chip 「混雑で進めない」;
   - the retry pin 「状況により車両規制」;
   - the sim panel's ruled-out row 「混雑する赤枠内を長く歩く」 (it said 「規制の可能性」);
   - EN-only: "Check road closures and crowds first", "SAVE THIS: Shibuya road closures".

   The red-frame caption 「赤枠＝車両の検問・規制エリア」 is unchanged. The zone scramble caption stays on one line
   (「スクランブル交差点：一歩も進めない」).
4. **alt: the last inner-loop trains end at Osaki.** The mission card now reads 「山手線の内回り／大崎行き（品川方面）で帰れ」
   (EN "Ride the Yamanote inner loop / to Osaki (toward Shinagawa)"). Its caption 「渋谷の内回り終電0:48は大崎行き」 carries
   the timetable source (JR-LAST-02). On the cheat sheet the header is 「内回り（大崎方面）」. The times are unchanged
   (Shibuya 0:48, Harajuku 0:45, Ebisu 0:50; normal weekend timetable).
5. **EN: no Japanese left on screen.** The RISK column of the sim table showed 「スコア 4:05」. That label came from a CSS rule
   (`ui/hud.css .gh-simp .sc::before{content:"スコア "}`). The v11-en check only read the DOM text (`innerText`), so it never
   saw the label, and it never saw text painted into the 3D scene either. Fixes:
   - **The label:** `body.lang-en … ::before{content:"Score "}`. The numbers are unchanged.
   - **The check now covers every text source of the EN page:**
     - `measureFit` reads CSS generated content (`::before` / `::after`) and SVG text.
     - The page logs every `fillText` / `strokeText` that carries CJK (`window.__cjkCanvas`). That covers the 3D signage,
       the boards, the neon and the LED ticker.
     - `record_variant.py --lang en` fails any EN frame with CJK in the DOM, CSS or canvas text. Allowlist below.
   - **The scan found Japanese in-world text the old check missed.** It is now drawn in English in the EN edition (same
     sign, same place): station signs and platform numbers, the taxi-stand and closed boards, the departure board, the
     Meiji-dori plate, the city neon words and the screen kana, the photo-spot / trash signs, the police DJ bubble and the
     LED ticker. JP pages are unchanged; every change is behind `body.lang-en`.
   - **Photo plates:** the browser scan cannot see text inside the photo plates. Every plate was checked by eye after its
     blur rects. Only the AI-made street plate `hachikomae` still showed legible Japanese shop signs (「もんじゃ」「クスリ」,
     vertical signs, small shop boards). It is fully visible in zone over-result / caught / over-lesson / continue and
     behind the opening cards. The EN edition blurs these with 5 extra rects (`tools/v11_composite.py EN_BLANK`, the
     usual `blank` treatment); the JP edition keeps the signs as on site. Only the EN segments showing that plate were
     composited again.
   - OCR of the rendered frames was not possible on this machine (no tesseract). The browser-side scan reads the actual
     text sources instead; the proof stills were checked by eye.
   - **Allowlist of Japanese permitted in the EN edition:** *empty* (`record_variant.EN_CJK_ALLOW = frozenset()`).
6. **EN title: one title everywhere = "ESCAPE FROM HALLOWEEN SHIBUYA!"** The 3D logo glyphs (`logo_glyphs_en.json`)
   already spell it, so only the header text changed. That was the cheaper choice: no new glyph build. The header is on
   every frame, so the EN videos were fully re-rendered.
   - **The longer title did not fit at first.** At 27 px it ran from x 63 to x 727 of the 720 px HUD, and `#gh` clipped the
     "!" in the first EN render. The fit check missed it because it skipped the frame-edge test for any box that crossed
     the edge (it took that for a slide-in). Now only a translated (sliding) box is skipped (`components.mjs measureFit`).
     The EN header is 22.5 px (`body.lang-en #gh .gh-head .l1`) and ends at about x 616. All EN beats were probed again
     with the stricter check (stride 6, 2,214 frames: 0 overflow), then the EN videos were rendered again from scratch.

  record is in `v111.fixes` of each config. Ghost text: `tools/v10_ghost.py TEXT_FIX` (JP), `tools/v11_ghost.py` (EN). EN
  table: `tools/v11_en_dict.py` → `i18n/en.json`.

  (`V111_GOODBYE_FACT0`). `V6_GOODBYE` stays as the copy of the v6–v10.2 videos.
- **JP (`tools/v111_ja.py`):** only the changed beats are rendered again, on the v10.2 pipeline:
  - **HUD:** the HUD of every beat whose text differs from v9. `v10_hud.changed_beats` now also sees the lesson and cheat
    cards and the retry "why".
  - **CG:** the changed CG segments are rendered fresh:
    - GOODBYE in every pair. v10.2 reused the v6 frames; it is now a CG render of the same beat.
    - alt over-start.
    - zone sim + retry.

  Every other frame is the v10.2 frame. The JP frames were rendered with `V111_FIT=1`, which measures text fit in JP too.
- **EN:** full re-render with `tools/v11_render.py` / `v11_composite.py` / `v11_finish.py` (`WORK11` = `v11.1/render-en`),
  after a probe pass of every beat (`probe-en.sh`).
- The run script is `logs/ssl-claude-halloween/v11.1/render-all.sh`. It is resumable through its marker files (`ja-hud`,
  `ja-cg`, `ja-ghost`, `finished-ja-<pair>`, `rendered-en-<who>`, `en-ghost`, `finished-en-<pair>`).
- The box rebooted mid-render (2026-10-29 18:45). The run resumed from its markers; the Python packages lost with

  rc=0 for two crashed probes (`$?` read after `$(date)`); this is fixed, and the zone / alt probes were completed.
