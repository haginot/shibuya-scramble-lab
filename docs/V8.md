# JR編 v8 — the crowd-flow simulation drives the route choice

v8 builds on v7 (`docs/V7.md`): the same four videos (`gate`, `taxi`, `zone`, `alt`), CG↔photoreal switching, TRUE END,
GOODBYE end card, footnote safe zone and −14 LUFS original audio. Brief: `logs/ssl-claude-halloween/v8/brief.md` and
`STEERING-sim-core.md` (HAGINO 2026-10-28: 「人流シミュレーションに基づいた最適経路選択的な要素がなくなってしまった。ここが肝なのに…」).
Each video is 97.7 s long (2931 frames), which keeps it under 100 s.

## What changed
1. **The sim segment** comes right after the start map (`sim-plan` 6.5 s → `sim-pick` 2.5 s, CG PLATEAU map in map mode):
   - **Heat map:** the crowd density is drawn as a heat map: amber → red, in people/m², with 4.0 as the solver's cap, from the solver's 3 m density cells.
   - **Agents:** every route walker is a coloured dot, and a 1/12 sample of the crowd appears as faint dots. All routes of the pair walk at once.
   - **Compare panel:** one row per route, with:
     - predicted time (m:ss);
     - the density bar and value (live while the agents walk, the route's peak once done);
     - risk 低/中/高 (peak < 2 / 2–3 / ≥ 3 people/m²);
     - the **score**.
   - **Rows:** they stay in A/B/C order while the sim runs, then slide into rank order. Routes ruled out by a sourced fact, or by the sim itself (not through within 15 min, or a missed last train), show ✕ with the reason.
   - **SIM PICK:** the sim highlights the argmin of the score in gold, with 「シミュレーションの結論」.
   - **On-screen rule:** 「スコア＝予測時間＋1分×（最大密度−2人/m²）」. The assumptions line is shown too (48,000 people at the peak; Hachiko gate entry metering assumed (2024 example); JR 2026 measures unannounced).
2. **The gut pick:** `over-choice` comes right after the SIM PICK. The player ignores the sim once (「シミュレーションの結論は「…」」 chip + 「でも…みんなが行く方でしょ？」), takes route A and gets caught.
3. **Retry uses the sim:**
   - `retry-why` (2.5 s): the heat map is frozen at the failing moment of the same plan run. A pulsing ring and a pin give the sim's numbers there; for gate that is 「改札前 4.0人/m² 15分たっても通れない」.
   - `clear-choice` (4.5 s): the **re-run** uses the crowd of the failing time slot. The rows start in the first ranking, fill with the new numbers and then re-sort, and the new optimum is highlighted (「再計算の結論」).
4. **`simmeter` on the move / result beats**, on the photoreal plates and on the CG maps:
   - It shows the density of the densest 3 m cell within 6 m of the route's median walker, as a 5-segment bar plus the value in people/m².
   - It shows the ETA to the route's end (sim, plus the OSM leg).
   - On the plates, the silhouette crowd scales with that density: how many figures appear, and how close they stand.
5. **Story follows the solver:**
   - **gate:** the sim picks the **New South gate** (South jams at 3.8 people/m²; Hachiko never clears the metered queue), so the CLEAR is now at 新南改札. The New South plate is used, with the 3F exits caption (JR-GATE-08/09).
   - **alt:** outcomes are swapped. The sim (walk legs plus OSM legs, regular Sat/holiday last trains) says Ebisu (later last train 0:50, but ≈ 28 min away) misses it, while Harajuku (0:45, ≈ 20.5 min) makes it from 0:23.
     - The gut pick is Ebisu (「終電が遅い方が安心でしょ？」) → GAME OVER.
     - The sim pick, Harajuku, → CLEAR.
     - The lesson becomes 「終電が遅い駅が正解とは限らない」.
   - **zone:** the solver alone puts the shortest route ≈ 30 s ahead. It is ✕ by a stated rule, 「赤枠内を長く歩く（規制の可能性）」 (MEAS-01: 2026 police traffic measures; 2026 pedestrian rules unannounced). The sim pick is the outer route.
   - **taxi:** the West-exit stand is ✕ by TAXI-01 (suspended since 2025-03-30); the sim times the walk to SAKURA Terrace.
6. **Kaiju** (`kaiju.mjs`):
   - The dorsal crystals cycle a non-blue party palette (pink / magenta / yellow / orange / lime; no hue in 170–260°).
   - The beam is replaced by a **confetti / party-cannon blast** into the sky: about 700 paper pieces, streamers and a pop flash, a pure function of time.
   - The new synthesised SFX `party_popper` sits at the pop (beat-local 1.95 s).
7. **Duration:** mission 3.5 → 3.0 s, first choice 4.5 → 4.0, guide 3 → 2.5, sources 3.5 → 3.0. The sources note adds the sim assumptions.

## The solver run (numbers on screen)
```sh
node tools/v8_sim.mjs all          # plan + retry scenarios (48,000 people, deterministic, cached by source hash; ≈ 6 min each)
node tools/v8_configs.mjs --write  # v8 configs from the v7 configs + the sim summaries
```
- **Model:** `web3d/shibuya/series/sim-v8.mjs` holds the route set, the scenarios and the scoring rule. The solver is crowd-jr.mjs on halloween.mjs (social force, 4 p/m² cap, scramble signal, gate metering). `config.jr.routeDefs` (new) runs the v8 routes; all 8 routes walk in the same crowd at once, with 24 walkers each.
- **Scenarios:**
  - `plan`: 150 s warm-up.
  - `retry`: 390 s warm-up, i.e. the crowd of the failing time slot.
  - Both use Hachiko gate metering of 0.45 people/s (assumption, after the 2024 restriction MEAS-15) and 3.2 people/s heading for JR.
- **Off-sim legs:** zone and alt leave the ≈ 380 m model. The rest of the OSRM foot route on OSM is added at 80 m/min and labelled 「OSMから概算」.
- **Outputs:**
  - `web3d/shibuya/series/variants/jr/v8/sim/{plan,retry}.json` (committed) hold the walker records, route numbers, scored rows, pick and the tracked walker per route.
  - `…/sim/cache/*-frames.json` (git-ignored) hold per-second density grids and positions for the heat map / dots.
- **Tests** (`tests/test_v8.py`):
  - The numbers are the medians of the walker records.
  - The pick is the argmin of the displayed score, both in the summary and in what the page hands the HUD (`panelState`).
  - The config texts (failing moment, arrival times) equal the summary.
  - After rendering, the logged SIM PICK frames show exactly the summary rows.

## Pipeline
```sh
python3 tools/render_v8.py plan <pair>       # layers: v7cg (frame-exact v7 CG), cg, photo, v6
python3 tools/render_v8.py cg|hud <pair>     # ≤300-frame segments, nice, browser lock, resumable, $STATUS file
python3 tools/v8_composite.py <pair>         # v7 photoreal layer + v8 HUD + sim-driven silhouettes + transitions
python3 tools/v8_finish.py all <pair>        # cues (+switch SFX) → mix −14 LUFS → mux → contact → verify (< 100 s)
```
- **Page:** `app-variant.mjs` loads the sim summaries and frames when a beat has `simmap` or a `simpanel` / `simmeter` item. The heat/dots layer is `series/sim-layer.mjs`, a 2D canvas between WebGL and the HUD that uses the beat camera's projection.
- **HUD components:** the new ones are `simpanel` and `simmeter` (`ui/components.mjs`, `hud.css`). The page's per-frame record logs them (`simpanel`, `simmeter`, `simmap`).
