v10 is v9 (`docs/V9.md`) with one change: the procedural flat costumed crowd is replaced by two photoreal Gemini plates
(approved by HAGINO) on the shots where it was most visible. Everything else is v9 frame for frame:

- the ghost lines and their timing;
- the audio cue sheet and the mix rules (−14 LUFS, −2 dBTP master);
- the cards, the sources and the PLATEAU / OSM credit.

## The crush POV on the photo (`tools/v10_shots.py crush_cam`, `tools/v10_composite.py crush_frame`)
- **Push-in:** a slow push from 0.90 to 0.72 of the plate height over the 8 s beat. The camera sinks into the crowd.
- **Sway:** 0.42 Hz + 0.17 Hz lateral sway, breathing bob and a ±0.45° roll.
- **Shoves:** on every shove of the config (`crush.shoves`, the same times as the shove SFX), the camera lurches forward
  (+3 %), gets an alternating lateral kick (26 px) and an extra roll. It also squeezes horizontally (3.5 %) and gets a short
  horizontal motion smear.
- **Grade:** desaturated shadows, 10 % darker, and a vignette that tightens with the heartbeat (0.74 s) and over the beat.
- **Framing:** the output corners never leave the photo (tested). A reflect pad is only a safety net.
- **HUD:** the v9 HUD layer is unchanged: the crushmeter climbs to the crush scenario's peak (gate 6.44, taxi 4.50, zone 4.67,
  alt 5.56 p/m²), 「圧死危険」 at ≥ 4, and the 「シミュレーション（仮定：…）」 label.

## Ghost placement (`tools/v10_ghost.py`)
- **What it checks:** the v9 lines and boxes are re-checked against the protect boxes of the photo (`protect`: the faces
  of the photo people and the blank sign panel). Each box is projected through the frame's camera on every 2nd frame of the
  line (pop-in / pop-out included) and dilated by 16 px.
- **What moved:** only boxes that touch a protect box are searched again, with `v9_ghost.search`. The order is protect
  first, then HUD core and HUD overlap, inside the same TikTok/Reels safe area. Two boxes moved, both gate:
  - over-result moved 6 px up;
  - crush POV moved 54 px up.
- **Result:** `web3d/shibuya/series/variants/jr/v10/ghost.json` (`protect` = 0 on every line; `moved` marks the two).
- **Refactor:** `v9_ghost.place` was split into `hud_mask` + `search`. The v9 placements regenerate byte-identically.

## Pipeline
```sh
python3 tools/v10_ghost.py place all            # ghost boxes vs the photo faces / panel → variants/jr/v10/ghost.json
python3 tools/v10_composite.py <pair>           # 600-frame segments in $WORK10/<pair>/seg (resumable) → video.mp4
python3 tools/v10_finish.py all <pair>          # v9 cues → mix → mux → contact → verify → <pair>-share.mp4 (crf 23, < 200 MB)
python3 tools/v10_ghost.py sheet <pair> out.jpg # QA: ghost lines on the new-plate shots, protect boxes outlined
```
`WORK10` defaults to `logs/ssl-claude-halloween/v10.2/render`. The HUD and CG inputs are read from the v9 work dir, except the v10.2 HUD frames (`python3 tools/v10_hud.py render all`, before the composite).
The run script is `logs/.../v10/render-all.sh`, which skips finished pairs and segments.
