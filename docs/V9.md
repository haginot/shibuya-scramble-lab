# JR編 v9 — Halloween costumes everywhere + the crush POV

v9 builds on v8 (`docs/V8.md`): the same four videos (`gate`, `taxi`, `zone`, `alt`), the same solver-driven story,
CG↔photoreal switching, TRUE END, GOODBYE end card, footnote safe zone and −14 LUFS original audio.
Brief: `logs/ssl-claude-halloween/v9/brief.md` and `STEERING-costumes.md`, `STEERING-crush-pov.md`, `STEERING-length.md`,
`STEERING-keep-v8.md`, `STEERING-guide-ghost.md`, `STEERING-ghost-voice.md`. Each video is 121.7 s long (3651 frames).

## What changed
1. **Costumes on every person** (`web3d/shibuya/series/costumes-v9.json`, one table for the page and the compositor):
   - 14 classes: zombie makeup (grey-green skin, torn hems), skeleton maid, pumpkin head, mascot suit (original
     generic animals), witch (hat, broom), vampire (cape), nurse with a cartoon red print, mummy, sheet ghost, devil, angel
     (halo, wings), cardboard robot, inflatable dino and an original fantasy outfit. Saturated orange / purple / lime /
     hot-pink / white-and-black palettes.
   - The class is a pure function of the person id (`classOf(id)`: the same 32-bit hash in JS and in Python, tested).
   - **CG crowd:** configs carry `look.costumes:'v9'`. `scene-jr.mjs` then builds the solver crowd, interior and
     platform people with `v9Style(i)`. The palette slot is encoded in the costume flags, so `costume.mjs` recolours the
     body (uniform `uV9`) and adds hats, ears/horns, capes/wings, skirts/sheets and brooms. Near people
     (`people-close.mjs setNearCostumes`) get the costume colours and pieces too.
   - **Sim map:** the crowd sample dots of the heat map take the colour of their costume (`simmap.costumes`). For that,
     `tools/v8_sim.mjs` now also stores the agent ids. The re-run gave the same numbers, scores and tracks.
   - **Photoreal plates:** `tools/costumes_v9.py` draws procedural costumed figures in place of the v7/v8 silhouettes.
     - Figures are seen from the back, or wear full costume heads; no face is ever drawn.
     - The head-count still follows the sim meter (the v8 rule), at about 2× the v8 crowd.
     - On `over-result` at ≥ 3 p/m², a front row of costumed backs presses into the bottom of the frame.
   - **Never attackers:** the costumed crowd only stands, sways or walks away. In `caught` it is a still bystander crowd,
     with no zombie-makeup costumes in that beat. The attackers are only the fictional game-zombie sprites (shadow masses
     plus three-fingered pixel-outlined hands from the frame edges, `attackers_layer`), whose sprite set and palette are
     disjoint from the costumes (tests).
2. **Crush POV** (`crush-pov`, 8 s, photo layer `mode:'crush'`):
   - **Picture:** six rows of costumed backs, edge to edge and pressed against the lens, with only a thin strip of night
     sky. The rows sway and are shoved at 0.95 / 2.25 / 3.35 / 4.65 / 5.9 / 6.95 s; the frame is squeezed horizontally on each
     shove; the camera barely creeps forward. Desaturated shadows and a vignette that tightens with the heartbeat.
   - **Sound:** original synthesis only — `crush_muffled` (low-passed crowd bed), `heartbeat`, `breath`, and `shove` cues
     on the picture's shoves. No BGM.
   - **HUD `crushmeter`:**
     - the crush scenario's real number (below), in 8 segments of 1 p/m²;
     - 「圧死危険」 pulsing at ≥ 4 p/m², which is the solver's own crush threshold (`JR_CROWD.crush`);
     - labelled 「シミュレーション（仮定：モデルの上限 4人/m² を 8人/m² に緩和）」.
   - **Placement:** gate / taxi / zone put it after `over-result`, right before the caught sequence. In alt it comes
     after the gut pick, on the way out (alt's GAME OVER is the missed last train).
3. **CLEAR contrast** (`clear-pov`, 3.0 s, photo walk): the CLEAR route at eye level, a few costumed people with room
   between them, plus the retry run's meter (< 3 p/m²).
4. **Longer where it carries information** (121.7 s vs 97.7 s); every extra second is reading time for the HUD and
   the ghost's bubbles (≈ 1.0 s + 0.12 s/char):
   - guide 2.5 → 3.0 s, over-start (start map) 2.5 → 3.5 s;
   - sim-plan 6.5 → 11.0 s (a slower fast-forward; three ghost bubbles: red = packed, dots = people, what is compared);
   - sim-pick 2.5 → 4.0 s (rows re-sort over 0.8 s, then the verdict holds about 2.8 s);
   - retry-why 2.5 → 5.0 s (adds 「同じシミュレーションの失敗した瞬間」);
   - clear-choice (re-run) 4.5 → 7.5 s;
   - the new crush POV (8 s) and CLEAR POV (3 s).
5. **Guide ghost 「おばけ」** (`tools/v9_ghost.py`, lines + placement in `variants/jr/v9/ghost.json`):
   - **Design:** original and procedural — a round white sheet with a scalloped hem, a tiny tilted orange party hat,
     big simple eyes, pink cheeks, stubby waving arms; it bobs and blinks. No name beyond 「おばけ」. It is a narrator
     only, and looks nothing like the costumed crowd (no sheet-ghost costume colours / sizes) or the game zombies.
   - **Voice (STEERING-ghost-voice):** a silly, slightly dim ghost who is a Shibuya nerd. Casual spoken Japanese, no
     jargon (ヒートマップ / エージェント / 密度 / ソルバー / 最適化 / スコア are banned and tested), short bubbles (≤ 2 lines ×
     20 chars), each shown for 1.0 s + 0.12 s/char with a quick typewriter. The crush POV lines are serious.
   - **Every scene:** mission, first choice / TRUE END, guide, start map, the sim in steps, the gut pick, GAME OVER
     lesson, retry-why (with the failing number), re-run, crush POV (with the scenario peak), CLEAR POV, result, cheat
     sheet, kaiju. It is silent in caught / GAME OVER / GAME CLEAR stamps and never appears on the GOODBYE card: it
     introduces the card neutrally at the end of the kaiju beat (「次は渋谷区のお知らせ」).
   - **Numbers:** quoted numbers come from the sim output (`4万8千人` = agents, crush `1m²にN人以上` = floor of the crush
     peak) or the sim-generated retry-why pin (tested).
   - **Placement:** `python3 tools/render_v9.py probe <pair>` renders the HUD layer alone every 6th frame of every beat;
     `python3 tools/v9_ghost.py place <pair>` puts each beat's ghost + bubble on the left inside the safe area
     (x 42–918, y 230–1536: not in the top 12 %, bottom 20 % or right 15 %) where the HUD is empty (dilated 12 px),
     lower-left preferred, bubble on either side. Overlap is 0 in every beat except SIM PICK, where the bubble touches a
     route pin marker on the map (panel rows / SIM PICK / meters / labels stay clear). `python3 tools/v9_ghost.py sheet
     <pair> out.jpg` draws the QA sheet. The overlay is drawn by the compositor on top of all layers.
   - **Sound:** 2–5 soft pitched blips per bubble (`ghost_blip_a/b/c`, synthesised; no TTS, no voice samples).

## The crush scenario (numbers on screen)
```sh
node tools/v9_sim.mjs crush        # deterministic, cached by source hash (≈ 7.5 min)
node tools/v9_configs.mjs --write  # v9 configs = v8 build() + the changes above
```
- **Scenario:** the v8 `retry` crowd (48,000 people, failing time slot, Hachiko metering 0.45 people/s, 3.2 people/s
  bound for JR, the same 8 routes × 24 walkers), with two changes:
  - the hard 1 m² occupancy limit is raised from 4 to 8 (a subclass passes it to `halloween.mjs move()`; the solver
    sources are unchanged);
  - the queue spacing (`queueStop`) is raised from 3 to 6.
- **Meter rule:** as the v8 `simmeter`, the densest 3 m cell within 6 m of the walker.
- **Which walker:** the worst walker of the GAME OVER route, i.e. the one who meets the densest cell. Its meter rows climb
  over the beat and end at its peak.
- **Output:** `web3d/shibuya/series/variants/jr/v9/sim/crush.json` (`pov`, `worst`, `track`, `numbers`).

## Pipeline
```sh
python3 tools/render_v9.py plan|cg|hud|probe <pair>|opening   # layers v7cg / v8cg (frame-exact reuse) / cg / photo / v6
python3 tools/v9_ghost.py write && python3 tools/v9_ghost.py place all   # ghost lines + placement
python3 tools/v9_composite.py <pair>                    # plates + costumes / crush POV / game-zombie caught + HUD + cuts
python3 tools/v9_finish.py all <pair>                   # cues → mix −14 LUFS (−2 dBTP master) → mux → contact → verify
```
- **v8cg:** reuses the v8 CG frames of beats whose picture and HUD are unchanged and show no CG person — over-choice,
  clear-move maps, kaiju, sources, and zone's CG over-move.
- **Re-rendered:** the cold open and the start map (costumed CG crowd), plus the sim and retry beats.

## Tests (`tests/test_v9.py`)
- **Costumes:**
  - the JS and JSON tables are identical;
  - assignment is deterministic, with JS == Python;
  - all 14 classes (≥ 8) appear;
  - no class is an attacker, and the sprite set and palette are disjoint;
  - the CG flags carry the class.
- **Crush meter:** the config value == `crush.json`, == what the page's `meterState` gives at the end of the climb, ==
  the rendered frames' `crushmeter` record.
- **SIM PICK:** still the argmin of the displayed score (plan and retry).
- **Story and configs:** the v8 story changes are kept, and the configs are generated and valid (100–130 s).
- **Ghost:** bubble limits and reading time, no jargon / banned words, every scene explained, silent beats, safe-zone
  placement for every beat, quoted numbers == sim output.
