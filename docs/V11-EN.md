## How the EN build works (one code path, a language switch)
- **Text**: the JP configs and components stay the source of truth. With `lang=en` (page URL; `tools/record_variant.py --lang en`)
  every displayed string is looked up in `web3d/shibuya/series/i18n/en.json` (generated by `tools/v11_en_dict.py`: one JP → EN
  table, exact strings + 4 number patterns). `GameHUD.frame` translates the frame state (`trDeep`) and the text between tags of
  the rendered HTML (`trHtml`, for the words hard-coded in `ui/components.mjs`). With `lang=ja` both are the identity:
  the JP HUD HTML of 976 sampled states is byte-identical to v10.2 (7ec8667), the only diff being the zone wrap fix (test).
- **Layout in EN**: the display-font fit (`fitFs`, end card, gag) measures Latin widths (Dela Gothic One metrics); the goodbye
  card shows the EN text once (no JP/EN double lines); tab sub-labels that only repeat the tab word are dropped ("SIM" +
  「シミュレーション」 → "SIMULATION").
- **3D logo**: `tools/build_logo_glyphs.py --lang en` → `web3d/data/logo_glyphs_en.json` / `logo_sdf_en.png` / `logo_halo_en.png`
  ("ESCAPE FROM / HALLOWEEN / SHIBUYA!" + "JR" ticket + "10/31 HOW DO YOU GET HOME?"; same OFL fonts, which have full
  Latin coverage). The JP logo files rebuild byte-identically.
- **Pictures**: every non-photo beat is re-rendered in 3D with the EN HUD (`tools/v11_render.py cg`). v10 reused older
  v6 / v7 / v8 CG frames for some beats, but their HUD is burnt in Japanese. The photo beats get an EN HUD layer
  (`tools/v11_render.py hud`), composited by `tools/v11_composite.py` (= `tools/v10_composite.py` on the EN layers, plus
  the EN AI-image tag on the crush POV). Same timeline (121.7 s, 3651 frames), configs, sim, plates, cuts and audio cues.
- **Ghost**: `tools/v11_ghost.py` (EN lines, boxes re-checked against the EN HUD), drawn by the same `tools/v9_ghost.py`.
- **Audio**: the JP build has no TTS or voice narration (captions + procedural SFX / music; the ghost's "voice" is
  synthesized blips). So the EN build is **captions-only** too: the same cue sheet and mix chain (`tools/v11_finish.py` =
  `tools/v10_finish.py`), −14 LUFS integrated, −2 dBTP master ceiling.

## Not changed
- Story, structure (TRUE END for not going; "Don't go to Shibuya Halloween"), beats, timing, sim runs and numbers, plates,
  costumes, crush POV, kaiju, cuts, SFX / music, the ghost's design and line starts.
- In-world 3D signage stays as on site. The station name boards are bilingual JP / EN like the real ones, and the taxi-stand /
  closure boards are Japanese props whose meaning is in the EN captions. Photo-plate signage was already blurred.
- The facts: every number, date and time is the JP text's, with the same source ids (JR-GATE-xx, …). Source names are the
  EN names of `sources-series.mjs`. Japan-specific places get one short gloss where first on screen: Hachiko ("the dog
  statue meetup spot"), the Scramble Crossing ("the famous all-way crossing"), "Shibuya-kei = 90s cool" (ghost). Last
  trains, Miyamasuzaka, Sakuragaokacho and the Yamanote loops are explained by their captions.
