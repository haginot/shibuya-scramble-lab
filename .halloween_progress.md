# Halloween mode progress

2026-10-21 — branch `halloween`, author HAGINO <hagino@local>.

## Scope and safeguards

Create a local, vertical 1080×1920 H.264 film (28 seconds planned), five PNG story frames, a reproducible recorder, and an actual deterministic crowd-routing comparison. Push only `halloween`. No publishing, visibility changes, social posts, or merges. PLATEAU footprint and height geometry stays unchanged. Characters, decorations, materials, particles and graphic design are authored procedurally for this project.
